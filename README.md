# DEX Trading Activity Dashboard

A Dune Analytics dashboard analyzing decentralized exchange (DEX) trading activity using SQL and blockchain data.

---

## Business Question

How has decentralized exchange (DEX) activity changed over the last 30 days, and which DEXs dominate trading volume?

---

## Dashboard Metrics

- Daily Trading Volume
- Daily Number of Trades
- Daily Unique Traders
- Top DEXs by Trading Volume
- DEX Market Share

---

## Key Insights

- Uniswap accounted for the largest share of DEX trading volume.
- Daily trading volume fluctuated between approximately $4B and $7B.
- Daily unique traders remained relatively stable around 600k–700k wallets.
- Trading activity is concentrated among a small number of major DEXs.
- Daily trade counts generally ranged between 10M and 15M transactions.
